<!-- atmosphere · RELEASE_NOTES.md · What's new in the next release; shown on GitHub and in the app's update dialog -->
A new settings menu, and albums that look the part.

- Settings is now a tabbed window that opens over whatever you're doing, so you never lose your place. Open it from the sidebar or with Ctrl+, and close it with Esc. It remembers the tab you were on.
- Find album covers online. Edit an album and choose Search online to pick a cover from TheAudioDB, Deezer, Apple Music or the Cover Art Archive. It's written into your files only when you save.
- Albums can have a logo and an About section. Atmosphere looks them up for you, or you can search for a logo, choose your own file or write your own description.
- Storage cleanup no longer removes artist logos, and backups now include logos and album info.
- Artist pages credit the right source for their bio.
- Home shelves now show one tidy row at any window size, and the window buttons stay sharp at every display scale.
