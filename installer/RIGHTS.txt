ATMOSPHERE: YOUR RESPONSIBILITY

Atmosphere saves audio and video you're allowed to keep, and plays your own music library.

By installing Atmosphere, you agree that:

1. You'll only download media you own, that the rights holder has allowed you to download, or that's under a licence that permits it, such as artist-enabled downloads or Creative Commons.

2. You understand that downloading without permission can infringe copyright and break a site's terms of use, and that you're responsible for how you use Atmosphere.

3. You won't share downloaded media unless its licence or the rights holder allows it.

Atmosphere never bypasses DRM or paywalls, and skips preview-only media. Artists recovering their own uploads can enable downloads on their tracks and choose "Original file" to get their uploaded master.

Atmosphere downloads two open-source tools on first run, both from their official releases with checksum verification:
- yt-dlp (Unlicense): https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
- ffmpeg (GPL builds): https://github.com/yt-dlp/FFmpeg-Builds

Clicking "I Agree" confirms these points, so Atmosphere won't ask again when you first open it.
